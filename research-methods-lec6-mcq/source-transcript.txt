MCQs Lecture 6 — Basic Research
Research Methods — Dr. Fayyad (the professor's own end-of-lecture MCQs; correct answer in bold in the source)

1. What is the primary purpose of basic research:
   A. To provide an immediate service
   B. To produce a commercial product
   C. To expand knowledge and understand fundamental processes [correct]
   D. To implement a treatment
   E. To address only urgent clinical needs

2. Basic research is also called:
   A. Clinical research
   B. Fundamental research [correct]
   C. Market research
   D. Operational research
   E. Evaluation research

3. Which question is most characteristic of basic research:
   A. Which treatment should a hospital adopt this year?
   B. How much does a new test cost?
   C. Which product should a company sell?
   D. How do cells communicate with one another? [correct]
   E. How should a service be delivered?

4. Basic research is:
   A. It always produces an immediate treatment
   B. Its practical value may emerge indirectly or after a delay [correct]
   C. It cannot contribute to practical advances
   D. It is useful only when it produces a commercial product
   E. It must solve a public health problem immediately

5. What determines whether a study is basic or applied:
   A. The disease or topic being studied
   B. The country where the study takes place
   C. The number of researchers involved
   D. The study’s purpose [correct]
   E. The equipment used

6. Which is an example of basic research in genetics:
   A. Investigating how gene activity is regulated [correct]
   B. Developing a screening service
   C. Comparing two treatments for a genetic disease
   D. Testing the cost of genetic counseling
   E. Introducing a new clinical guideline

7. How immune cells recognize pathogens to design a vaccine is an example of:
   A. Basic research
   B. Applied research [correct]
   C. Comparative research only
   D. Observational research only
   E. Theory development

8. Which statement about research methods is correct:
   A. Basic research must use laboratory experiments
   B. Basic research uses only mathematical models
   C. Basic research cannot use observations
   D. Basic research is defined by its use of microscopy
   E. Basic research may use different methods depending on the question [correct]

9. Which method can help researchers observe biological structures and processes:
   A. Survey design
   B. Random assignment
   C. Microscopy [correct]
   D. Policy analysis
   E. Cost evaluation

10. In basic research, a mechanism is:
   A. A practical recommendation
   B. A research cost
   C. A type of participant
   D. A process or series of events that explains how something happens [correct]
   E. A final report section

11. What is a hypothesis:
   A. A broad explanation supported by substantial evidence
   B. A proposed explanation that can be tested [correct]
   C. A simplified representation of a system
   D. A list of research methods
   E. An established practical solution

12. What is a theory, as defined in the lecture:
   A. An untested guess
   B. A method for collecting samples
   C. A broader explanation supported by substantial evidence [correct]
   D. A type of laboratory equipment
   E. A single observation

13. Which is a possible strength of basic research:
   A. It guarantees a treatment will be developed
   B. It always has an immediate effect on clinical care
   C. It may reveal previously unknown processes [correct]
   D. It eliminates the need for further studies
   E. It ensures all findings can be generalized

14. Which is a limitation of basic research:
   A. It cannot produce new knowledge
   B. It cannot use reliable methods
   C. It is always unethical
   D. It may require substantial time and resources [correct]
   E. It cannot lead to later applications

15. How basic research may contribute to a practical advance:
   A. Implement a treatment, then identify a mechanism
   B. Develop a product, then decide what question it answers
   C. Identify a mechanism, test the finding further, develop a possible solution, and evaluate it [correct]
   D. Publish a theory, then skip testing
   E. Choose a treatment before gathering evidence

16. Does a basic discovery guarantee that a useful treatment will be developed:
   A. Yes, every discovery leads to a treatment
   B. Yes, if it involves a disease
   C. Yes, after one additional study
   D. No, further research is needed, and a useful treatment is not guaranteed [correct]
   E. No, because basic research cannot support treatments

17. Researchers study how a virus enters a cell. A later team investigates whether blocking that entry can prevent infection. The first study is primarily ___, and the later study is primarily ___.:
   A. Applied; applied
   B. Applied; basic
   C. Clinical; theoretical
   D. Basic; applied [correct]
   E. Observational; comparative

18. Which is an example of basic research in physiology:
   A. Comparing hospital care plans
   B. Designing a new blood pressure monitor for sale
   C. Evaluating a clinic’s patient services
   D. Testing a new treatment in patients
   E. Investigating how the kidneys regulate water and salts [correct]

19. Which is an ethical responsibility in basic research:
   A. Reporting only results that support the hypothesis
   B. Avoiding the disclosure of uncertainty
   C. Using biological materials without safe handling
   D. Protecting participants and animals, where relevant [correct]
   E. Changing methods without recording the changes

20. Which practice supports scientific reliability:
   A. Ignoring results that conflict with expectations
   B. Using appropriate controls and reliable methods [correct]
   C. Reporting only favorable findings
   D. Avoiding analysis of the evidence
   E. Hiding study limitations

21. Why may basic research lead to unexpected findings:
   A. It avoids asking research questions
   B. It does not collect evidence
   C. It studies only known treatments
   D. It explores how processes work and may reveal results beyond the original question [correct]
   E. It is designed to confirm every existing explanation
