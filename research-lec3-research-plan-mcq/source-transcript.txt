MCQs Lecture Three — Designing the Research Plan
Principles of Research — Dr. Linda Munjad Hadi — (the professor's own MCQ file; correct answer highlighted/bold in the source)

1. What is the main purpose of a research plan:
   A. To list only the study references
   B. To present results after a study is complete
   C. To replace the research question
   D. To map how a study will be done [correct]
   E. To guarantee that a hypothesis is correct

2. When should a research plan be prepared:
   A. Before data collection begins [correct]
   B. After statistical analysis
   C. After the final report is written
   D. Only if results are unexpected
   E. After recruiting all participants

3. Which feature best describes a good research question:
   A. Broad and interpretive
   B. Clear, focused, measurable, and feasible [correct]
   C. Written as a conclusion

   E. Limited to available results

4. How does a research objective differ from a research question:

   B. An objective identifies the sample size
   C. An objective states what will be done to answer the question [correct]

   C. To identify the study budget
   D. To determine the final conclusion

   B. Exposure is assigned and outcome is measured later
   C. Participants are followed for several years
   D. Only after the outcome occurs

   A. Whether they have the outcome

   C. Their laboratory result quality

   B. Observational studies always use surveys
   C. Experimental studies cannot include human participants

   B. The participants actually enrolled
   C. The accessible group at one site
   D. The people who completed follow-up

   B. Use them to remove results that do not support the hypothesis

   B. A larger sample always gives a better study
   C. It supports adequate precision while considering feasibility [correct]

15. Which factor may increase the sample size needed:
   A. A smaller expected difference
   B. Lower precision requirements

   A. Inclusion criterion

   B. The factor selected for investigation

18. Which sequence best describes a planned data and specimen workflow:
   A. Collect, publish, recruit, calibrate, consent
   B. Analyze, recruit, collect, report, store

20. When should the data analysis plan be decided:
   A. During participant consent
   B. After examining all final results
   C. Only after an unexpected finding
   D. After the conclusion is written
