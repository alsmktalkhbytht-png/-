1. Which is research:
   A. A way to confirm what the researcher already thinks
   B. A personal belief about a topic
   C. A collection of unrelated facts
   D. A systematic investigation to answer questions, solve problems, or discover information [correct]
   E. A discussion without collecting data

   A. Question → Investigation → Data → Analysis → Answer [correct]
   B. Answer → Question → Data → Investigation → Analysis
   C. Data → Opinion → Answer → Question
   D. Observation → Conclusion → Data → Question
   E. Analysis → Investigation → Question → Answer

3. Scientific research is :
   A. Unverified observations
   B. Personal experience alone
   C. Popular opinions and assumptions

   B. To ensure a preferred treatment appears effective

   A. Systematic approach [correct]
